Frequently asked questions

How do I report a waste concern?

Open Submit a waste report, choose the issue type and barangay, add a description and photo, then submit. You can track the report under My Reports.

Where can I check a report's status?

Open My Reports to view saved submissions, their current status, and any photo attached to each report.

How do I change my profile photo or details?

Open Profile, choose Edit Profile, select a photo if you want to change it, update your details, and save your changes.

Why are my settings or reports only on this device?

This is a browser-based demo. Profile details, preferences, reports, and demo password data are kept in this browser and are not sent to a city server.

What if I forget my demo password?

The demo has no account recovery service. Clearing saved app data in Settings resets the local password, but also removes reports and other data stored by this demo.

For urgent collection or sanitation concerns, contact your local barangay office. This demo does not send support requests to city staff.
